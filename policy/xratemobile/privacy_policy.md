# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 2026

Komil R. from CODE.TJ ("we," "our," or "us") operates the XRate mobile application (the "App"). 

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
We built this App to provide exchange rate information for the Tajikistani Somoni (TJS). 
* **Personal Data:** We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location). 
* **Permissions:** This App requires only standard internet access permissions (`android.permission.INTERNET`) to fetch exchange rates from an open API. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or device storage.

### 2. Third-Party Services
The App connects to a public, open API to fetch current currency conversion rates. While our App itself does not collect any data, the third-party API provider may log standard network requests (like your device's IP address) to facilitate the data transfer. We do not control these third parties, and we encourage you to review the privacy practices of the open API provider used by the App.

### 3. Data Safety & Security
Because we do not collect any user data through the App, we do not store or process your data on any external servers. The security of your experience is important to us, and the App relies on secure network connections (HTTPS) to fetch currency data.

### 4. Children's Privacy
Our App does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
